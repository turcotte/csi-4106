# Message of the day (MOTD)

CSI 4106 Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

**Message of the Day (MOTD)** refers to the informational message presented to users upon logging into a system. This message typically includes updates on system modifications, availability, and other relevant announcements. In Unix-like operating systems, such information is commonly stored in the file located at `/etc/motd`.

At the beginning of each class, I dedicate a brief segment to presenting a current topic that has caught my attention. I ensure to vary the themes to reach as wide an audience as possible, while emphasizing local and national information. By the end of the semester, we have usually reviewed the main players in the field.
